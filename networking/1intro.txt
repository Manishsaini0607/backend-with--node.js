💻 What is Computer Networking?
Before diving into computer networking, let’s first understand the basic concept of a network.

🌐 What is a Network?
A network is a group of interconnected devices (like computers, smartphones, servers, printers, etc.) that are linked together to share data and resources.
🔗 Key Points:
Devices in a network are called nodes.
Networks can be:
Wired (using physical cables like Ethernet)       
Wireless (e.g., Wi-Fi, Bluetooth)

🖥️ What is Computer Networking?
Computer Networking is the field and practice of designing, building, and managing these device networks.
It enables computers and other devices to:
🗣️ Communicate with each other
📁 Share files
🌍 Access the internet
🖨️ Use shared resources like printers and servers

✅ Summary
Computer Networking is essential for modern computing — it connects devices, supports communication, and powers everything from your home Wi-Fi to global data centers.
